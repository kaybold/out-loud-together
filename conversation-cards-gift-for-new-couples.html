Are Conversation Cards a Good Gift for a New Couple?

You’ve been seeing someone for a few weeks, and now you’re trying to buy them a holiday gift. You know their coffee order. You know which show they keep telling you to watch. You’re still figuring out what kind of gift feels right.

We think conversation cards can be a good choice if you already enjoy talking and want something to do on your next night in. The easiest way to tell is to read a few prompts and picture asking one over dinner. Would it make you curious about their answer? Could you answer it yourself without feeling put on the spot?

Look for a question you’d ask on a regular date

We’d look for something like, “What did you think we’d have in common when we first met?” or “What’s something you could talk about for twenty minutes if I asked the right question?” You’ll probably get a story out of either one. If the questions you see are mostly about moving in or planning the next five years, save that deck for a time when you’re both in the mood for those conversations.

Check how the cards talk about relationships

If you’re buying for a queer couple, read a few actual cards before you decide. We wanted Out Loud Together to make room for different ways people meet, date, and build a life. Early on, that can be as simple as giving someone space to say what a good date feels like to them. You could ask, “Where would you take me if you were planning a date entirely around something you love?” The answer might be a bookstore, a roller rink, or the quiet booth at their favorite diner.

Give it with an invitation

Our favorite way to give a deck to someone you’re newly dating is to make it part of a plan. Write, “Dinner at my place Friday? You pick the first card.” If you draw a question you’d rather save for later, set it aside and keep eating.
